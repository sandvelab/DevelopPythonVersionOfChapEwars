### CH-15

inla.posterior.sample defaults (posterior.sample.R:375-379): use.improved.mean=TRUE (means from the VB correction, control.vb enable 'auto'), skew.corr=TRUE (skew-normal correction of the Gaussian samples). Hyperparameters are drawn from the integration configurations with probability proportional to exp(log posterior), without the integration weights (posterior.sample.R:488-496). Latent samples come from N(mean, Q^-1) at each configuration under the constraints (inla.qsample). inla.mode defaults to 'compact'; num.threads to '8:1'.

Raised 2026-10-14, batch 3.

### CH-19

predict.R draws rnbinom(mu = exp(Predictor)), which is the NB mean only if the offset is included. If it were not, the R reference's samples would be on a per-unit-population scale, and the port must match whichever is true. Raised 2026-10-14, batch 3.

### CH-20

Evidence, all in analysis/03_referenceHarness/results/. (1) Across 5 refits of each dataset x variant on byte-identical model frames, the posterior mode of the cyclic-RW1 log precision ranges over 1.77-2.04 (monthly default), 1.43-1.70 (monthly no_covariates), 2.65-2.85 (weekly nlags_3_6); the NB log size and iid log precision vary far less (fit_summary.tsv, refit_agreement.tsv). Monthly fitted values differ by up to 472 cases between refits. (2) One of 42 sweep fits (monthly default, the seeded repeat of replicate 1) converged to a degenerate mode: NB size 1.34, iid precision 290, 4 integration points instead of 15, six 'vb.correction aborted' warnings, mode_status 0 (seeded_repeat.tsv). (3) Three single-threaded fits per dataset (thread_diagnostic/) also differ from one another; on weekly data one crashed ('The Newton-Raphson optimizer did not converge') and two converged to degenerate modes. Threading is therefore not the cause. (4) Through chap, monthly repeat 1 produced no evaluation: INLA crashed with the same Newton-Raphson error in one backtest split (chap_repeats/service_container.log). Untested candidate causes: randomness inside INLA's optimiser or its rescue paths; the amd64 emulation on Apple Silicon (no native amd64 host was available). Consequences: the L3 noise floor is not a small numerical tolerance but a spread with occasional gross outliers; and an R-vs-R comparison at L3-L5 has to decide whether a degenerate R fit is part of the reference's behaviour or excluded from it — a decision for the human.

Raised 2026-10-15, batch 4.
